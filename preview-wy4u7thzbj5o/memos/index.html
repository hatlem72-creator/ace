Typed upstairs, dated, and left on the paste-up.

To: All staffFrom: The Ad ManRe: The ashtrayDate: 12 March 1974

The ashtray in reception is for clients. The plant has been moved two inches. This is not a discussion.

To: The Ad ManFrom: Ellis BrennanRe: LunchDate: 13 March 1974

The union opinion about lunch is that there should be one. I have checked.

To: All staffFrom: The Ad ManRe: New York GrooveDate: 4 April 1974

I hum “New York Groove” until a panel starts to work. We have not voted. Until we do, the humming continues.

To: CreativeFrom: Connie VossRe: Wood veneerDate: 9 May 1974

Wood veneer may be a promise. The paste-up will be square. I have a ruler.

To: The Ad ManFrom: ReceptionRe: The toasterDate: 21 June 1974

A man asked if we could “give a toaster the Ace treatment.” I said we do not. Debbie has the brochure.

To: All staffFrom: Chuck Daley’s officeRe: The tieDate: 2 July 1974

Mr. Daley would like it known that the tie is not a joke. The doughnut was for atmosphere, and was eaten off-camera.
