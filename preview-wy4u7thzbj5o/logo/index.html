The End… now go and draw something

What really happened
  • Paul Stanley came up with the name. All four original members have credited Ace Frehley with the first sketch of the logo.
  • Ace said he drew it the week the band picked the name, and put it on a homemade button with a felt-tip pen. His version had a diamond-shaped dot over the “i”.
  • Gene Simmons remembered asking Ace to put the band’s name on a giant photo for a club window and “make it look fancy”.
  • Ace said the jagged letters were lightning bolts, like the ones down the side of his first boots.
  • Paul, a trained artist, refined the sketch into the final version, drawn on his parents’ sofa with a straightedge and a drafting pen. He says he eyeballed it, so the two last letters are not exactly alike.
  • Ace’s verdict, years later: it “wasn’t really that difficult”, and the result is “probably number three” among rock logos.
The small print

You won’t find the real logo anywhere in this comic: it belongs to someone with very good lawyers. BIZZ is our invented stand-in, mismatched Z’s and all. The all-night pile of drafts, the wall of tries, the cat, the cheering crowd, the limo dream and the billboards are our comic take. NIKKY, Coca-Cooler and Apfel are invented parodies, not real brands.

Ace Frehley (1951–2025) died on 16 October 2025. Thanks for the sketch, Spaceman.
