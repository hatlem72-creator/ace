The End… or is it?

What he actually said
  • He lived in Yorktown, Westchester County, New York, which he called “considered an alien hotspot”.
  • He often saw objects in the sky above his Westchester home. One went “across the sky, like super fast”, then “stops dead and goes straight up”.
  • His first sighting, a “cigar-shaped something”, was from a plane while on tour with KISS.
  • One morning he woke up lying in his doorway, “halfway in the house and halfway out of the house”. He had been drinking, but he had never done that before.
  • In the front yard: a circle. In 2009 he called it “like this circular burn on the grass”. In 2024 he described “a depression, about 30 feet, a circle”.
  • He kept quiet about it, because he “didn’t want people on [his] property”.
  • A couple of weeks later he started dreaming about being inside a UFO. He put the chance he had been abducted at “50/50”.
Up in the sky

The sightings found their way into his music: the song “Up in the Sky” on his 2024 album 10,000 Volts was inspired by his close encounters.

Ace Frehley died on 16 October 2025. He left the question open, the way he liked it. Keep watching the skies.
