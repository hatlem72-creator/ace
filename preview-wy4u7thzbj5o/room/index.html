What really happened
  • In his memoir, drummer Peter Criss describes the mid-70s road years as long and lonely, and says one way the band fought the boredom was to break stuff.
  • They had heard the legendary stories of a famous drummer’s hotel pranks and set out to top them.
  • Hotel TVs went over the edge on long lengths of cable, and sinks and toilets were left to overflow onto the floors below.
  • The road crew carried plenty of tools. One night a sledgehammer went through a hotel wall to make “our own connecting door”.
  • Lobby paintings went missing, and a crossbow got so out of hand that it had to be taken away.
  • At a hotel with a beautiful golf course, they let the handbrake off the business manager’s rental car. It rolled down the hill and across the course.
  • The police traced the car to the business manager, and the band had to pay for the damages.
  • In the end hotels asked for a $10,000 deposit against any damages.
The small print

No golfers were harmed. The real crossbow was confiscated; ours shot suction cups. The rubber-duck pool party, the dukes’ new noses and the gags along the way are our comic take on the chaos.

Ace Frehley (1951–2025) died on 16 October 2025. Rock on, Spaceman. Mind the deposit.
