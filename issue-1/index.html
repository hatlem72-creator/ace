Shock Me!

The night the stage tried to electrocute the Spaceman

December 12, 1976Lakeland Civic Center, Lakeland, Florida100% True
Shock-o-meter9/10

The true tale

Picture it: a sold-out Lakeland Civic Center, 10,000 fans, and KISS making their grand entrance down glittering metal staircases for “Detroit Rock City”. Ace, never famous for his balance, reaches for the handrail to steady himself.

Big mistake. The railing wasn’t grounded. His hand plus his plugged-in guitar completed the circuit, and for a few long seconds the Spaceman was locked to the stage set by pure electricity, high up on a staircase above a wall of Marshall cabinets. Then he toppled, luckily backwards rather than face-first, and the road crew dragged him off the stairs.

Did the show stop? Briefly. Did Ace go home? Absolutely not. He sat in the dressing room for about ten minutes, then marched back out to a standing ovation with the crowd chanting his name. He played the rest of the show with half his fingers still numb. (That same night, for good measure, Gene set his hair on fire.)

His bandmates suggested he write a song about it. The result, “Shock Me” on 1977’s Love Gun, was the first KISS song Ace ever sang lead on. He was so nervous he recorded the vocal lying on his back in a darkened studio.

You may have read this happened in 1974. The concert records put it on December 12, 1976.
