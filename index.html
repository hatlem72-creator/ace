Ace Is Back! True Tales

A non-commercial fan tribute to Ace Frehley (1951–2025), founding lead guitarist of KISS. Not affiliated with or endorsed by KISS, the Frehley estate or any label. All illustrations are original artwork that shows scenes and objects only, with no likenesses, makeup designs or logos.

True means sourced: every story links its sources. Anything that rests only on Ace’s own telling gets a Legend stamp.
